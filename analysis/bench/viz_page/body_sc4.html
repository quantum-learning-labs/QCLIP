Readout cost figures

How accuracy, gate count, shot cost and energy per decision scale with circuit size, for the post-selection (PS) model and two discard models. All models have 2 ansatz layers. Size grows along two axes: more output qubits e (1 qubit per noun wire), or more qubits per noun wire n (3 output qubits).
