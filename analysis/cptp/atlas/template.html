One circuit, two readouts

Both models run the same parametrised unitary U on 3 sentence qubits S and m other wires E (m = 4 for 90% of sentences). They differ only in what closes the E wires.

Where the gap went

Test std hard-negative accuracy, 3 text layers. Plain discard starts 6.1 points behind post-selection; supervision from the post-selected model recovers 3.7 of them.

Mixing caps what discard can see

The traced sentence state ρ is mixed because S is entangled with E. Its Rényi-2 entropy S₂ = −log₂ Tr ρ² measures that entanglement (0 for a pure state, 3 bits for I/8).

Shots per correct decision

On hardware each score is a swap-test estimate. Post-selection throws away all but p₀ of its shots, but its margins are 5–7× larger, so it still needs fewer shots per correct decision from about 70% accuracy up.

Under realistic noise the order flips at m = 8

The honest profile (FakeMiami errors ×1.5–3, halved T1/T2, ZZ over-rotation, idle decay) matches unscaled 2025–26 IBM calibrations.

The failure branches carry discard's decision

Theorem A: a CPTP channel Φ reproduces every post-selected image-choice decision. Trained discard models do not find it; they use the k ≥ 1 branches instead.
